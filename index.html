The Agency

Polished presence.
Unforgettable impressions.

Founded by entrepreneur Nicole Tagge, VVS Models is a premiere promotional modeling, event staffing and talent agency built on one idea: the right people in the room change everything about how your brand is remembered.

Since 2018 our models, brand ambassadors and performers have worked nightlife venues, casinos, resort pools, car shows, trade shows and music & video productions across the West Coast — including regular staffing at the Grand Sierra Resort and Casino in Reno.
